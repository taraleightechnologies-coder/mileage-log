Businesses

Each business keeps its own trips, expenses, receipts, exports and printed reports. Switch between them with the Business menu at the top.

Saved places

Home, work and other places you drive to often. In the trip form, tap a saved place to fill in its address. GPS trips that start or stop near a saved place use its name.

IRS standard mileage rates

Each trip uses the rate in effect on its date. In 2026 the IRS raised the business rate mid-year: 72.5¢ for Jan 1–Jun 30, then 76¢ from Jul 1. You can edit these or add new rates when the IRS announces them.

Backup & restore

Your entries and receipt photos are saved inside this browser on this device only. Download a backup regularly (for example monthly) and keep it somewhere safe such as OneDrive or Google Drive. A backup can also be restored on another device to move your log there.
